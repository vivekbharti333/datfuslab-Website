Manage the complete donation lifecycle in one place

Record donor details, track donations, organise campaigns and generate receipts through a consistent workflow. Centralised records help your team spend less time searching across spreadsheets and more time maintaining meaningful donor relationships.

    Create user profiles and assign role-based permissions for administrators and staff. This helps your NGO keep responsibilities clear and restrict sensitive records to authorised users.

Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know about Donexia

Quick answers about donor management, 80G receipts, payments, reporting and getting your NGO started.

What is Donexia NGO CRM software?

Donexia is a cloud-based NGO CRM and donor management system that helps organisations manage donors, donations, campaigns, receipts, communication and reports from one secure platform.

Can Donexia generate 80G donation receipts?

Yes. Donexia can generate professional donation receipts with the donor, donation and organisation details required for your workflow, including 80G-related information where applicable.

Can receipts be sent automatically to donors?

Yes. NGOs can generate receipts after recording a donation and share them with donors through supported email, SMS or WhatsApp communication options.

Which payment gateways can be connected?

Donexia supports integrations with leading payment gateways such as Razorpay and Cashfree. Available integrations can vary by plan and implementation requirements.

Is Donexia suitable for small NGOs?

Yes. Donexia is suitable for small NGOs as well as growing and multi-team organisations. Plans and access controls can be selected according to the number of users and operational requirements.

Can staff members have different access permissions?

Yes. Role-based access lets an NGO assign suitable permissions to administrators, staff members and other authorised users while keeping sensitive donor information controlled.

Can donation and donor reports be exported?

Yes. Donexia provides reporting for donors, donations, transactions and campaigns, with export and sharing options for operational reviews and stakeholder reporting.

How can our NGO get started with Donexia?

Create an account through the free-trial form or contact Datfuslab Technologies. Our team can help you choose a plan, configure your organisation and begin importing donor and donation records.

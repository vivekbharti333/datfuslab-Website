Questions before you choose

Clear answers about setup, management scope and costs.

Can you migrate my existing website?

Yes. We can plan the transfer of website files, databases and domain settings after reviewing the current hosting environment.

Is SSL included?

SSL setup support is included in the listed plans. Certificate type and renewal terms are confirmed in the final proposal.

What happens if my website needs more resources?

We can review usage and recommend an upgrade or a server solution when shared hosting is no longer suitable.

Are domain and email charges included?

Domain registration, premium email and third-party licences are separate unless explicitly included in your proposal.

Need a dependable environment for your website or application?

Share your workload, current setup and support expectations. We will recommend an appropriate hosting or server-management scope.

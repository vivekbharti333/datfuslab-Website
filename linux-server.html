Questions before you choose

Clear answers about setup, management scope and costs.

Which Linux distributions do you support?

Support commonly includes current Ubuntu, Debian, AlmaLinux and compatible server distributions, subject to application requirements.

Can you manage an existing Linux server?

Yes. We first audit access, services, resource usage, backups and known issues before agreeing on a management scope.

Do you provide the cloud or VPS server?

We can assist with selecting and configuring infrastructure. Provider charges are separate unless included in the proposal.

Is emergency support included?

Response times and emergency coverage depend on the selected support plan and are documented before service begins.

Need a dependable environment for your website or application?

Share your workload, current setup and support expectations. We will recommend an appropriate hosting or server-management scope.

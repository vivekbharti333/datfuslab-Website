This policy applies to, but is not limited to:

  • CRM software purchases (including MyDonation CRM / NGO CRM)

  • AMC (Annual Maintenance Contract) charges
  • Website development and software development fees
  • Mobile app development fees
  • Digital marketing services
  • Bulk SMS/WhatsApp credits or service fees
  • Custom integrations and API setup charges

Clients are responsible for reviewing the product or service requirements before making any payment.

Request Demo Before Purchase

Since no refunds are provided, clients are strongly advised to:

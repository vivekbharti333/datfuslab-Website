Common questions

Bulk SMS, explained clearly

Still unsure about routes, credits or integration? These answers cover the essentials.

Ask another question →
What types of SMS can I send?

You can send transactional alerts, OTPs, reminders and consent-based promotional messages. The correct route and template depend on the purpose of the communication.

Can SMS connect with our CRM or application?

Yes. API integration can trigger messages automatically from lead, order, payment, appointment and account workflows.

What does one SMS credit mean?

Credit usage depends on message length, character encoding and destination. Long messages can use multiple SMS segments, so final estimates are confirmed from your content and volume.

Do you help with DLT requirements?

We provide practical setup guidance for entity, sender ID and template preparation. Approval remains subject to the relevant telecom and DLT platform processes.

Ready to improve your business messaging?

Tell us about your audience, campaign goals and integration needs. We will recommend a practical setup for your organisation.

WhatsApp business messaging, explained

Ask another question →
Can I message any WhatsApp number?

No. Businesses should message people who have provided valid opt-in, and must provide a clear way to stop receiving communication. We help design consent-aware workflows.

Why are approved templates required?

Business-initiated messages generally use approved templates. Template category and content affect how the message can be used and how platform charges are applied.

Can WhatsApp integrate with our CRM?

Yes. APIs and webhooks can connect lead capture, order status, payment reminders, appointments and support workflows with WhatsApp.

Are Meta conversation charges included?

Our displayed service fees do not include variable WhatsApp platform or provider charges. We confirm these separately based on category, destination and provider.

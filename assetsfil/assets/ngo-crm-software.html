Impressive feature

Streamline and Centralize All Your Donations

Easily manage the full donation lifecycle—from receiving funds to generating receipts. Track donor details, monitor status, and organize campaigns with real-time updates and secure records. Save time, reduce errors, and build donor trust with streamlined, transparent processes.

  • 1. User Management

    Create and manage user profiles with role-based access controls. Easily assign permissions for admins, staff, volunteers, and donors. Ensure secure, organized, and accountable system access across your organization.

  • 2. Donation Management

    Effortlessly create, update, and monitor donations in real-time. Manage donor details, donation types, statuses, and generate automatic receipts. Streamline donation campaigns with centralized control and complete transparency.

  • 3. Payment Gateways

    Integrate leading payment gateways like Razorpay, Cashfree, and more. Enable secure, fast, and multiple-mode payment options. Automatically track payment statuses and simplify reconciliation with real-time notifications.

  • 4. Report Management

    Generate dynamic, real-time reports on donations, users, transactions, and campaigns. Customize and export reports in multiple formats. Share reports via email or WhatsApp to keep stakeholders informed and engaged.

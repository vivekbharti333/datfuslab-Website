Common questions

Email marketing, explained clearly

Know what is included and what your team should prepare before starting.

Ask another question →
Do you provide the contact database?

No. Campaigns should use your permission-based customer or subscriber list. We can help clean, organise and segment the data you are authorised to use.

Can you design reusable email templates?

Yes. We can create responsive branded templates that your team can reuse for newsletters, announcements, offers and lifecycle communication.

Which results will I be able to track?

Available reporting commonly includes delivery, bounces, opens, clicks and unsubscribes. Conversion measurement depends on your website and analytics setup.

Are sending-platform fees included?

Platform fees are separate unless explicitly included in a proposal. They depend on contact count, monthly sending volume, functionality and provider.

Ready to improve your business messaging?

Tell us about your audience, campaign goals and integration needs. We will recommend a practical setup for your organisation.
